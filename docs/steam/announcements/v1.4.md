=== en
Title: V1.4 — Full Graphics Overhaul
Subtitle: Bosses and menu art are redrawn, and the screen is cleaned up so enemies, attacks and your skills stay readable in a crowd.
Body:
[h2]🎨 Redrawn Art[/h2]
[list]
[*]All 6 bosses (King Slime, Lich King, Demon Lord, Developer, Mirror Sovereign, Mom) are redrawn at the same pixel size as heroes and enemies. Bosses no longer look like giant pixels.
[*]The final boss "Mom" now has her own look.
[*]All 9 hero portraits and 20 skins in the main menu are redrawn.
[*]Ability and Soul Tree icons now share one consistent style.
[*]Coins, healing potions, the aim marker and the Lich King's ritual objects are redrawn.
[/list]
[h2]👁️ A Clearer Screen[/h2]
[list]
[*]Floor patterns and brightness are toned down so enemies no longer blend into the ground.
[*]Enemy attack warnings use a red rim, while your attacks use your hero's color. The confusing spinning runes under enemy warnings are gone.
[*]Your ground effects and coins are drawn below enemies so they no longer hide them.
[*]Enemy outlines are cleaned up, and enemies dim slightly when hit instead of flashing red.
[*]Small damage numbers no longer pile up over crowds you hit at once. The damage number font is also bolder and clearer.
[*]The huge floor patterns of Spatial Erosion and boss domains are now thin rings.
[/list]
[h2]⚙️ New Setting[/h2]
[list]
[*]Settings → HUD "Enemy HP Bars": Elites Only (default) · All Damaged
[/list]
[h2]🔧 Bug Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fixed the whole screen being covered in color when a boss remembered a skill.
[*]Banner notices are smaller and placed higher so they cover less of the screen.
[/list]
[p]Thanks for playing![/p]
