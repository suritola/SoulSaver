# Soul Saver Gameplay Data Collection & Privacy Policy

Document version v1 · Effective 2026-10-20

<!-- Same content as the in-game consent text (Assets/Scripts/LocTable.Telemetry.cs). Update both and raise ConsentVersion when changing. -->

To make the game better, Soul Saver sends an anonymous gameplay record at the end of each run **only if you agree**. If you do not agree, every feature of the game works exactly the same, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > General.

## 1. What we collect

- Game version, full / demo edition, platform (operating system type), game language
- Hero, difficulty, mode, run result, play time, level, kill count
- Cards, weapons, skills, paths and evolutions chosen, time per chapter, damage dealt/taken statistics, cause of defeat, soul shards
- Ultimate, chapter reached, soul weight, brands and relics, skill evolutions, path tiers and crossovers, contract and milestone progress, retry type, time spent in menus between runs, weak-point hit statistics, other gameplay counters (score, whether herald or memory events occurred, etc.), consent document version
- Date (day only)
- A random anonymous ID (a random number the game creates when you first agree; it is not linked to your account or device)

We do not store your name, SteamID, email or IP address. Due to how the internet works, your IP address is processed briefly during transmission, but it is not stored or logged.

## 2. Purpose

Used only for game balancing, bug fixing and performance improvement. Not used for advertising, sales or profiling, and never sold or provided to third parties.

## 3. Legal basis

Your consent (Korean Personal Information Protection Act Art. 15(1)(1), GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

## 4. Retention

Automatically deleted 2 years after collection, or without delay when you request deletion or the service ends.

## 5. Processors and international transfer

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Recipient | Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) · contact privacyquestions@cloudflare.com |
| Countries | United States and other countries where Cloudflare data centers are located |
| When & how | Sent over the internet (HTTPS encrypted) at the end of each run |
| Data transferred | The items in section 1 |
| Purpose | Data storage and hosting |
| Retention | Same as section 4 |
| How to refuse & effect | Choose "Decline" or turn it off in Settings; nothing is sent. Refusing has no effect on your use of the game |

Transfers of data of EU/UK residents rely on Cloudflare's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## 6. Your rights

- You may refuse consent and withdraw it at any time in Settings > General. After withdrawal, nothing more is sent.
- You can request deletion of the records sent so far with the "Request deletion of my data" button in Settings > General.
- You can request access, correction or restriction of processing via the contact below.
- You may lodge a complaint with the Personal Information Protection Commission of Korea or, if you live in the EU, with the supervisory authority of your country.

Records are identified only by the anonymous ID, so please include the ID shown in the Settings screen with any request.

## 7. Children

If you are under 14 (under 13 in the United States), do not agree without a parent or guardian's consent — please choose "Decline".

## 8. Security

All transmissions are encrypted with HTTPS, and access to stored data is restricted to the developer.
