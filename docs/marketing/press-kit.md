## Description (short, 50 words)

A survivors-like with no auto-aim. Aim, reload, draw the bow. Possess foes as the Ghost, rewind time as the Clockmaker: nine heroes, nine hands. Only aimed hits strike a boss's weak point, and bosses learn to block your top move. Beat one in its barrier and your weapon evolves on the spot.

## Description (long)

When the dead rose from the Meadow, the King Slime swallowed the fields and the sleepers beneath the earth began to stir. Pick a hero and descend from the Meadow into the Catacombs, the Burning Hell and the Abyss of Souls.

There is no auto-aim: you aim every shot and reload by hand. Hundreds of enemies close in from every side. Shoot, slash and blow them apart, level up, and pick from cards that shape your run. Defeat a boss and your weapon shatters — then evolves into something completely new: a revolver becomes a Soul Sniper, then a Soul Railgun. Every hero has their own evolution paths, their own right-click ultimate and their own branches in the Soul Tree.

Fight through the Meadow, the Catacombs, the Burning Hell and the Abyss of Souls, take down four kings with cinematic entrances and enraged phases — including the Mirror Sovereign, who copies your own skills — then unlock the Burning Desert Endless Mode where every monster and boss keeps coming.

## Key Features

- **Weapon evolution on every boss** — two irreversible choices per run, different paths for every hero
- **100+ node Soul Tree** — Weapon, Ultimate, Fate, Survival, Soul and Wealth branches
- **Nine heroes, nine hands** — Gunner · Swordsman · Rogue · Archer · Alchemist · Cartographer · Clockmaker · Postman · Ghost, each with a different verb
- **Weak points and bosses that remember** — only aimed hits strike a weak point; at set health, bosses learn to block your top attack (from the weak point & memory update)
- **Four worlds, four kings** — the King Slime that shrinks and speeds up as you wear it down, the Lich King, the Demon Lord and the Mirror Sovereign who copies your skills
- **Endless Mode** — every monster and boss, faster and faster
- **Skin Shop** — 20 character skins, 10 weapon skins, 4 effect skins and cursor skins
- **4 languages, 38 Steam achievements**
